# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge
We are committed to creating a respectful, harassment-free community for all contributors.

## Expected Behavior
- Be respectful and constructive.
- Assume positive intent.
- Provide actionable feedback.
- Focus on technical merit and collaboration.

## Unacceptable Behavior
- Harassment, discrimination, or abusive language.
- Personal attacks or intimidation.
- Publishing private information without consent.

## Enforcement
Maintainers may remove or edit content and restrict participation for behavior that violates this policy.

## Reporting
Report incidents privately to repository maintainers.
